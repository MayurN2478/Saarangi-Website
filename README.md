# Saarangi – The Vintage Kitchen (Mysuru)

A faithful, modern, elegant and fully responsive recreation of the official website for **Saarangi – The Vintage Kitchen** located in Vijayanagar, Mysuru, Karnataka ([saarangi.in](https://saarangi.in)).

---

## 🏛️ About the Restaurant

* **Origins & Legacy:** Traces its culinary roots back to **1998** in the sacred **Narayanagiri area of Tirumala (TTD)**.
* **Mysuru Launch:** Grand opening on **22 June 2025** in Vijayanagar 4th Stage, Greater Mysore.
* **Specialty:** Authentic **100% Firewood Cooking (*ಸೌದೆ ಒಲೆ ಊಟ*)**, bringing unmatched rustic aroma and slow-simmered flavors.
* **Capacity:** Spacious **340+ guest capacity** across **6 distinct seating experiences**.
* **100% Segregation Protocol:** Strictly independent kitchens, washing bays, utensils, and service staff for Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian cuisine.
* **Chaat Ka Chaska:** Dedicated pure vegetarian counter serving legendary Old Delhi street food and chaats.
